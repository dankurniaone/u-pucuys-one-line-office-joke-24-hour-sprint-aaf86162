## The joke

> Our office plant has survived three rounds of layoffs, two CEOs, and a merger — HR just gave it a retention bonus.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) validates the line against the gate — 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt` and reads it back to verify the round-trip.
